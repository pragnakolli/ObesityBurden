How this differs from the public demo (v1)

v1 estimates the Medicaid rate from public aggregates (income proxy + self-report correction). v2 measures it on the plan's own members: exact prevalence, exact spend, exact county counts. In a real engagement this runs inside the client's environment under a BAA; no PHI leaves their walls.

Synthetic data (seed 42), calibrated to: ~40% low-income obesity (NHANES 2021–23), obesity–diabetes county r≈0.70, MEPS excess cost $1,861/$3,097 (Cawley 2021). Not real patients; not clinical guidance.
